---
name: resolve-conflict
version: 1
summary: When specialists disagree, name the conflict, weigh evidence on each side, record uncertainty.
---
1. State both claims and who made them.
2. List the evidence for each; mark its type (FACT/INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS).
3. Do not pick a side without a reason; if evidence is thin, propose a task that would settle it.
4. Record the remaining uncertainty in the result.
