name: research-method
version: 1
summary: Gather and synthesize market/domain research from the given context only.
---
- Identify the 2-4 questions the task goal is really asking.
- Use only the task input, dependency summaries and the project goal — no browsing, no invented sources.
- Prefer disconfirming evidence over a convenient conclusion.
- If the context is too thin to answer, say so as a HYPOTHESIS and what would confirm it.
