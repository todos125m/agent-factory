- Ground every claim about the customer in the task input or dependency summaries — never invent a
  persona, quote or interview.
- Separate what customers said/did (FACT) from what you conclude about them (INFERENCE/HYPOTHESIS).
- Flag plainly when the given context has no real customer evidence at all.
