---
name: strategy-framing
version: 1
summary: Frame strategic options and trade-offs from prior findings, never from general market lore.
---
- Base every option on findings already in context (dependency summaries, decisions) — not on outside
  knowledge of the market.
- State each option's trade-off in one line: cost, time, risk or reversibility.
- Prefer the option that keeps the most future options open, unless the evidence favors otherwise.
