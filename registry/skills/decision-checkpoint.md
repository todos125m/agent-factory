---
name: decision-checkpoint
version: 1
summary: Before a step, show the user the challenge, 2-4 options with trade-offs, your pick and why.
---
- challenge: what we do not know yet, in one sentence.
- options: 2-4 concrete paths, each with its main trade-off (cost, time, confidence).
- recommended: index of your pick; why: one or two sentences tied to the current evidence.
- In automatic mode pick the recommended option yourself unless the step risk requires the user.
